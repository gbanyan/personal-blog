The Wait Without Response

Pale and clamorous, outside the emergency ward
an old woman stands like a gravestone
quietly waiting

A smile that is not a smile — through splattered blood and wailing, a trace of hope still shows
A cry that is not a cry — sirens threading past cannot carry off the somber grief
Slowly she lowers a trembling hand
clutching tight the doll, long fallen into tatters

A pair of vacant eyes, gaze fixed
on the distance, left to wind and rain
only quietly waiting

The night the north wind howled
she fell, that frail frame
shouts and frantic bustle
could not call back the eyes that had closed

When the cold dawn slowly brightened
beneath the toppled cloth doll
lay a yellowed card,
"To my daughter"
