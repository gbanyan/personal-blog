Birth, death
Perhaps are merely
The two ends of a tightly bound
Throbbing, humming
Yearning for a soulmate, yet hoping to break free

Shall we play a tune together? You slightly tremble
The agonizing tremble of fingertips sweeps across the strings, glides past the ear
Glides past the intertwining bonds of countless words

But I gently let it cease
Letting the lingering echoes slowly drift away
Perhaps, harmonizing is also a kind of bondage
Let us choose to part ways
