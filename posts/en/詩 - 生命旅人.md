By the fireplace, the sleepless flames

The traveler's cloak, waiting in silence

Once swept through the red maple, the autumn that engulfed the earth

Drunk and flush with wine, the mountain mist surges, snowflakes scattering down

Not quite a wandering knight, nor a singing bard

Only wanting, quietly, to leave without taking a single flake of frost

Time flows on, remaining as before.
