Found a guide I wrote in my own notes on how to stop overspending, so I'm posting it here to share.

* Purpose: To stop myself from impulse buying and establish a quantified basis for assessing purchases.
* Necessity Assessment: Prioritize, or see if it fits multiple criteria simultaneously:
  1. Is it immediately needed for work, research, or study?
  2. Health-related.
  3. Related to skill exploration.
  4. Entertainment or interpersonal relations.
  5. Lifestyle decoration or aesthetics.
  6. Just wanting to explore a new use or function?
* Do I already own a similar item?
  + Is the wear and tear of the old item to the point where it is about to break?
  + Are the existing functions of the old item no longer sufficient?
  + Will replacing it with a new item increase life efficiency?
* Prioritize items that have been on the list for a longer time and have undergone careful planning.
  + Items not on the list are just a spur-of-the-moment whim.
* Price Range:
  + Set a basic spending limit for a single month.
  + For items with a unit price exceeding a certain value, do not concentrate multiple purchases in one month.
  + If it is a planned purchase and the unit price exceeds 10,000 (NTD), make good use of installment payments to spread out the cost.
